Deploy anything to your own server. Keep it boring.

Dockyrd is a lean, self-hosted alternative to Coolify. One Go binary turns a fresh VPS into a platform for Next.js apps, static sites, Postgres, Redis and S3 storage, with automatic HTTPS and zero-downtime deploys.

What you get

Everything a small team ships, on one server you own.

Dockyrd covers the services most projects actually need, with one consistent screen for each. No feature wall, no plugin store.

Applications

Push to GitHub and Dockyrd builds with Railpack or your Dockerfile, health-checks the new container and only then switches traffic. Failed deploys never replace a working one.

Built to stay up

Restarting the panel never touches your traffic.

The control plane and the data plane are separate. Caddy, your apps and your databases keep running while Dockyrd updates, crashes or restarts.

  • Blue/green deploys. The new container must pass its health check before Caddy switches the upstream.
  • Self-healing. A reconciler compares what should run with what does every 30 seconds and repairs drift.
  • Signed updates. Releases are checksummed and cosign-verified, with automatic rollback if the new version fails to start.
  • Volumes are sacred. Nothing automatic ever deletes a volume. Only an explicit, confirmed delete does.

Under the hood

Three moving parts. That is the whole platform.

Every container Dockyrd creates carries labels, so Docker itself is the source of truth for what is running. SQLite holds what should be running.
